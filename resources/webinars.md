# Webinars

## ARSET Course: Harnessing NISAR

_July 2026_

NASA's [Applied Remote Sensing Training (ARSET)](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/arset) program offered [Harnessing NISAR: Next-Generation Radar Observations for Earth Applications](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/trainings/harnessing-nisar-next-generation-radar-observations-earth-applications) in July 2026. The course was offered in both [English](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/trainings/harnessing-nisar-next-generation-radar-observations-earth-applications) and [Spanish](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/es/learn/trainings/nisar-nuevas-observaciones-de-radar-para-aplicaciones-terrestres). 

This training course introduces users to the characteristics and data of the NISAR mission and demonstrates workflows for accessing, visualizing, and analyzing the data for a range of applications. 

Recordings of each session are available for reference. Visit the course site for the [English version](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/trainings/harnessing-nisar-next-generation-radar-observations-earth-applications) or [Spanish version](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/es/learn/trainings/nisar-nuevas-observaciones-de-radar-para-aplicaciones-terrestres) to access the recordings, presentation slides, and other resources.

## NISAR Science Team Meeting

_February 2026_

Leaders of the NISAR project held a [virtual town hall and data access webinar](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/webinars/nisar-science-team-town-hall-data-access-webinar) during their Science Team meeting in February 2026. 

- The town hall offers updates on the mission status, science, data products, and upcoming data releases. 
- The data access webinar, which starts at the 44:48 mark in the video, provides information and resources for accessing and analyzing NISAR data.
